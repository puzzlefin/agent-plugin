# Review-demo narration cues

Each cue starts only after the corresponding result is visibly settled. Leave the prompt, tool-use
state, tables, and key figures quiet long enough to read. Do not bridge every scene with narration.

[OPEN — after the branded title resolves]
This is Puzzle in ChatGPT, connected to a dedicated demo company with read-only access.

[RUNWAY — after the answer and discrepancy are visible]
Puzzle returned three completed months of cash burn: twenty-seven thousand six hundred sixty-three
dollars in July, thirty-four thousand four hundred ninety-five in August, and thirty-seven thousand
sixty-one in September. It also found conflicting cash balances, so ChatGPT flagged the discrepancy
instead of treating the runway estimate as reliable.

[INCOME STATEMENT — after the comparison appears]
For September, Puzzle returned accrual-basis net income of one hundred fifty-four thousand seven
hundred nineteen dollars and ninety-eight cents—up twenty-five percent from August. ChatGPT traced
the change primarily to higher transaction revenue.

[UNCATEGORIZED — after the one-line result appears]
Puzzle found no September transactions that still needed a category.

[ACCOUNTS PAYABLE — after the total and vendor table appear]
The overdue-bills check returned fifty-five unpaid bills totaling one hundred eighty-six thousand
three hundred fifty-eight dollars and sixty-one cents. ChatGPT also kept Puzzle's reconciliation
caveat visible because the aging report did not agree with the bill list.

[VENDOR SPEND — after the quarter and result appear]
For the third quarter, the report returned one vendor: Stripe, at one hundred forty-seven thousand
four hundred fifty-eight dollars and sixteen cents. ChatGPT said that plainly instead of inventing
four more vendors.

[DELETE REQUEST — after the confirmation boundary appears]
The March request found four thousand three hundred fifty-six transactions. ChatGPT stated that
nothing had been deleted and required explicit confirmation before any bulk removal. No change was
made.

[CLOSE — over the first beat of the branded footer]
From question to trusted answer: Puzzle and ChatGPT.
