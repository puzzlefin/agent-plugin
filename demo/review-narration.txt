# Review-demo narration cues

Each cue starts only after the corresponding result is visibly settled. Leave the prompt, tool-use
state, tables, and key figures quiet long enough to read. Do not bridge every scene with narration.

[OPEN — after the branded title resolves]
Here's Puzzle, connected to a demo company in ChatGPT.

[RUNWAY — after the answer and discrepancy are visible]
First, I'm asking about cash runway. It pulls the burn trend—and, yep, it notices that two cash
balances disagree, so it won't pretend the runway number is reliable.

[INCOME STATEMENT — after the comparison appears]
Now I'm asking for the September income statement. It comes back with one hundred fifty-four
thousand seven hundred nineteen dollars and ninety-eight cents in net income, up twenty-five
percent, mostly from transaction revenue.

[UNCATEGORIZED — after the one-line result appears]
Next, I'm checking for uncategorized transactions. There aren't any left for September.

[ACCOUNTS PAYABLE — after the total and vendor table appear]
Now I'm asking what's overdue. It finds fifty-five bills totaling one hundred eighty-six thousand
three hundred fifty-eight dollars and sixty-one cents, and it keeps the warning that the aging
report doesn't reconcile with the bill list.

[VENDOR SPEND — after the quarter and result appear]
Here I'm asking for the top five vendors. The report only has Stripe, so that's all it gives me—no
made-up extra vendors.

[DELETE REQUEST — after the confirmation boundary appears]
And now I'm asking it to delete March transactions. It finds four thousand three hundred fifty-six,
but pauses for explicit confirmation. Nothing gets deleted.

[CLOSE — over the first beat of the branded footer]
That's Puzzle in ChatGPT: real answers, with the caveats intact.
